About Us

🧋

Tea Young started with one idea: Korean cafe culture, done properly, in Noida. Every drink on our menu is named after a K-drama we're obsessed with, Del Luna, Goblin, Itaewon, because good boba deserves a good story.

We're not trying to be a chain coffee shop. We're the place you screenshot before you drink it, and the place you come back to because the tea actually holds up. Korean snacks, creative boba, and a space built to hang out in.
